# TASK E — Process lessons from veri7vn for the VerEKOS sibling

**Summary (5 lines).**
1. veri7vn ran on one law — **0 wrong** — enforced through a scoreboard built from the upstream project's *own* panels and scorers; everything else (proofs, builds, fixtures) was explicitly declared "not numbers" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/AGENTS.md:9-12, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:88).
2. What worked: one golden family copied file-for-file by every lane, a frozen export recipe (HOL → SML → MLton, marker last, oracles=0), end-to-end ownership, and named-file commits with numbers — it produced 144 specialist implementations, one 161-reader relation kernel at 16,284/0 (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:50-54).
3. What went wrong: completion claims outran receipts (a universal zero-wrong claim the receipts did not support), a revision-2 infrastructure OS that no lane needed and was deleted, build-lock contention, and green proofs that proved the wrong model.
4. The transferable core is the trust boundary (proved kernel / untrusted recognizer+fetch+scorer, coverage as explicit premise) and the receipt discipline (immutable captures, hashes, drain manifests), not any Oracle7 content.
5. The scoreboard itself may not transfer: Oracle7 shipped per-component panels, scorers and panel generators; a knowledge compiler sibling must author its scoreboard before rule 1 can exist at all.

## 1. What worked (practice → evidence)

- **Scoreboard before proofs.** The completion metric is the upstream's shipped panels scored by the upstream's own scorers under `upstream/workbench/exam.py`'s six criteria (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/AGENTS.md:9-12, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:27-34). The harness only counted as a scoreboard after reproducing Oracle7's published `verdict_heldout.json` numbers: reproduced control 14,667 right / 17 false yes vs published 15,478 / 14, with the 811-answer delta explained as a store gap (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:44, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:30-35).
- **One golden family, copied file-for-file.** Lane briefs point at `src/extract/quantity/` and lane G's report as "THE pattern you copy file for file" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:10-18). Evidence it scaled: acoustics first at 69/0 (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:48), then one `quantity` kernel serving six specialists at 320/0 (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:50), then one relations kernel serving 161 readers at 16,284 right / 0 wrong of 18,976, beating the shipped organs' 16,261/11 on the same store (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:53, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:49). Fusion reproduced 101 of 102 of Oracle7's composed answers (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:51).
- **Beyond-parity as a second scoreboard with a fixed recipe.** `BEYOND.md`'s five steps (fresh generator seeds → reachable misses → coherent extension → efficiency → assurance) produced chemistry 281/0/0 vs the shipped organ's 223/2/56 and finance 129/0/0 vs 116/0/13, cold starts 58/270 ms → 4/3 ms (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:52, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/BEYOND.md:19-43).
- **Trust boundary in the type signature.** Kernel = HOL function from typed request to typed outcome with a binding theorem; recognizer and store fetch are labelled untrusted; "Where a decision needs all relevant rows, its proof depends on an explicit completeness premise" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:102-108, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/AGENTS.md:26-29).
- **Frozen export recipe.** `export_code ... in SML` inside the export theory, MLton (ships inside Isabelle), `Code_Target_Numeral` always, PASS marker the LAST command with `oracles=0`; the reader session prints the SHA-1 of the generated ML so the binary is tied to its session receipt (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/src/isabelle/reader/ROOT:1-6, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/src/isabelle/reader/Relation_Reader_Export.thy:8-29, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:117-121, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:95-97). Session `Veri7vn_Reader = HOL` is deliberately minimal (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/src/isabelle/reader/ROOT:1).
- **Binary provenance.** Immutable compiler-input captures; delivery archives with SHA256, byte sizes and 144-folder counts independently verified by root; a later edit never inherits an earlier score (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:36-43, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:302-304).
- **Performance as a scoreboard column.** 19 binaries in 12 MiB, median 0.1 ms/question vs Oracle7's head: 3.6 s start, 140 ms median, 1.96 GB resident (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:62-65).
- **Audit-first root probes after the swarm drained.** Root's own 30-observation efficiency receipt found 8 wrong numerical answers that green ideal-formula proofs had not (e.g. 20 instead of 16 N·m gear torque), and Fleet's `trip_cost_def` was shown to "prove that reduced model, not the original price unit" ($14.41 for a $12 trip) (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:662-669, 738-741). Post-drain single-owner repairs then closed them with receipts: Mythology 71/33/93 → 158/0/39 on the same 197 requests (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:254-255), Fleet 39/39 after typed unit roles with proof `53e322301edf`, 99 statements, zero oracles (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:216-219), mechanics efficiency proof 98 statements/zero oracles, native `45a99852` (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:855-856).
- **Honest bookkeeping compounds.** The assurance audit classified all 2,234 theorem statements (63% binding, 27% definitional, 15 vacuous) and wave 6 fixed the families (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:38-40); the wrap-up verified every preserved draft byte-for-byte against pushed branches (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/WRAPUP-2026-10-18.md:44-48).

## 2. What went wrong (quotes from the project's own admissions)

- **Completion claims the receipts did not support.** "The previous summary said every family had zero wrong answers. The underlying receipts did not support that claim." (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:56-57). "The universal zero-wrong claim does not follow from the family receipts; the registry omitted finance as well as earth" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:7-8). The handoff header now carries a standing correction: "some of its completion claims were stronger than the receipts support" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:8-9). Concrete counter-receipts: two wrong held-out answers in `var/stores/parity6/` (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:42-47).
- **A false green.** "W6-05's handoff claimed a first-attempt green that did not reproduce (five proof steps failed; W6-06 repaired the scripts, statements unchanged). Treat any 'green' without a fresh remote-build receipt line as a claim." (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:84). Same for scouts: "Reports are good; builds claimed green are not receipts." (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:108-110).
- **Proxy metrics that looked like results.** "W5-09's 924 unchanged replies compare two dispatcher versions, not 924 matches to the head." "The 236/237 conversation scout figure scores recorded Oracle7 replies; the scout kernel's own figure was 161/237." "Relation panels contain no invalid questions, so their zero-wrong figure does not measure false yes." (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:59-64, 67-68).
- **The revision-2 removal.** The first architecture — "Scala host, authority machine, durable journals, recovery, Forget, search service, package lifecycle, deployment generations, browser workbench" — "was removed from the tree on 2026-10-17 after no lane needed it" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:72-80); its era is visible as a run of WIP infrastructure commits (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/.git/logs/HEAD:53-58). Its first package scored "0 / 0, every panel item unrecognized" because nothing spoke the answerer contract (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:47).
- **Build-lock contention.** One local lock "shared by about twenty lanes" with instructions to write the whole family before the first build and never poll (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:66-70). Remote: "Someone is building `Veri7vn_Computing` on persvati from `~/veri7vn-builds/wt-computing` with the old no-lock script; it corrupted the shared session database twice" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:83-84); "persvati's shared memory cap kills builds under load" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:92-93); orphaned build groups could only be stopped "by ember's say-so" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:106-107).
- **Code-generator defaults that ate the box.** Without `Code_Target_Numeral`, nat/int export as unary: "lane S10 saw a binary use 6.5 GB on an empty input and a build climb past 30 GB" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:117); at the very end, "Code's 22-root proof passed, but factorial execution timed out because generated arithmetic uses unary naturals" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:108-109).
- **Proofs that proved the wrong thing.** "The existing ideal-formula proofs do not resolve the first two interpretation gaps" (efficiency, requested units) (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:648-651); `trip_cost_def` "proves that reduced model, not the original price unit" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:740-741). Also held-after-counterexample binaries: geometry `223516e` "not selected for delivery" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:289), Stores E replacing D because "D's scientific mass wording can silently use a default serving" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:299-303).
- **Proof-pass/execution-fail and staged-not-integrated.** Space's final proof "failed on order coercion", Document's "on the code-export attribute" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:114-116); Chemistry J passed everything "but remains staged"; Trades H had "a passed 51-statement proof and no native" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:60-63).
- **Composition gaps invisible per-family.** "W5-11 shows that a head can overwrite an organ's correct refusal" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:60-62); the generic unit reader overriding the physical specialist's negative-fabric refusal only appeared in full-default mode (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:173-176).
- **Usage cliff forced the endgame.** The drain was "requested at 40% account usage remaining" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:490), then a wrap-up with the goal "paused" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/WRAPUP-2026-10-18.md:3-6) — the drain/preservation machinery had to be invented mid-flight.

## 3. The sibling pattern, distilled (15 rules)

1. **Name the scoreboard before the first theorem.** It is the upstream project's own evaluation artifacts scored by its own scorers; "Compilation, a green theory and a passing fixture are not numbers" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/AGENTS.md:9-12), and "A claim about behavior comes with a panel number or it is not made" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:88). A harness that has not first reproduced the upstream's published numbers "is not a scoreboard" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:56-57).
2. **Keep one invariant law, move the rest.** 0 wrong never moves; parity is scoreboard one, "beyond" is scoreboard two (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/BEYOND.md:3-7, 57).
3. **Draw the trust line in types, not prose.** Proved kernel = typed request → typed outcome with a binding theorem; recognizer, fetch and scorer are untrusted and say so in their source; any "all rows" claim needs an explicit coverage premise (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:102-108, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/AGENTS.md:26-29).
4. **Build no infrastructure ahead of a lane that needs it.** veri7vn deleted its entire revision-2 OS after "no lane needed it"; deployment stayed "an extracted binary plus the SQLite stores" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:72-84).
5. **Lift one family end-to-end first, then freeze it as the golden example.** Every later brief points at the finished family's real files — ROOT, kernel, export, recognizer, build.sh, score.py — to copy file-for-file (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:10-18).
6. **Freeze the export recipe.** `export_code` → SML → MLton; `Code_Target_Numeral` in every exported theory; PASS marker last, `oracles=0`; two build attempts per lemma, then weaken or defer with a named `remaining` (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/src/isabelle/reader/Relation_Reader_Export.thy:8-29, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:55-57, 117, 121).
7. **Tie every binary to its inputs.** Print the generated-code hash in the build; compile from immutable captured inputs; hash, size and folder-count every archive, verified by root, as a fixed cohort (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/src/isabelle/reader/Relation_Reader_Export.thy:13-29, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:21-43).
8. **No verifier at answer time.** Ship small binaries plus data; builds run remotely through one driver that holds the one lock and returns a receipt; "a build counts as green only when its receipt reports success" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:110-111, 158-161, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:89-90).
9. **Transcribe data tables as constants with a drift checker.** HOL constants plus `tables_check.py` asserting equality against the upstream's own table (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:46-50).
10. **Lanes own exact paths; root integrates.** The brief names owned paths, the ground-truth file shapes, the build/score commands, and quotes the box-safety block verbatim; at ~420k context the lane writes `CONTINUE.md` and stops (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:28-40, 86-109, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/AGENTS.md:16-19).
11. **Commit named files, one landing per commit, with its number.** "[lane id]" prefixes; no `git add -A`; root pushes. The late history is exactly this: `[J-chemistry]`, `[H-trades]`, `[K-runtime]`, `[G-mechanics]`, `[L-fleet]`, `[G-space]`, `[M-mythology]`, `[G-relations]` (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:23-25, 93-95, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/.git/logs/HEAD:428-441).
12. **Owners are end-to-end, not scouts.** "Deputies own completed capabilities, including proofs, recognizers, measured behavior, performance, documentation, and integration with their consumers… No read-only scout farm." (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:72-75); "Owning a route binary is not integrating it" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:58).
13. **A handoff names: changed files, theorem statements (not names), the check that ran and its exact result, what remains, and the consumer** (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/AGENTS.md:21-22); "Static theorem names are not per-answer evidence" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:110-112).
14. **Every number ships with what it does not measure.** Scope lines on the board, every miss classified, refusal distinguished from capability: "Passing a refusal check is not an answered capability" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:41-54, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:8).
15. **Pre-register the drain.** When usage or attention ends: stop successors, integrate only tested packets, preserve unfinished source on pushed branches with `project/parked/` manifests (paths, hashes, limitations) and a resume map naming "exact paths, passed and failed receipts, current outstanding work, and a practical resume command" (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/DRAIN-2026-10-18.md:4-14, 98-106, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/CODEX-2026-10-18.md:528-530, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/WRAPUP-2026-10-18.md:29-48).

## 4. Specific to Oracle7, unlikely to transfer to VerEKOS

- **The free scoreboard.** Every upstream component shipped `manifest.json`, an `answer()` organ, a panel JSON, a `*_scorer.py`, and `verdict_heldout.json`, plus 151 panel generators for free fresh panels (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:28-40, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/BEYOND.md:9-17). VerEKOS's upstream (ledger + query answers) presumably ships none of that; authoring the panels/scorers *is* the first project phase, and rules 1-2 stand on it.
- **The organ control row and the drop-in package.** "Shipped organ" comparison rows and the 125→144-folder gift targeting Oracle7's `root/specialists/` depend on a live host with a uniform per-component answer contract (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:69-80). A ledger's consumer interface will need its own packaging shape and its own definition of "false yes" (answering what the ledger cannot support).
- **Numeric-exact machinery.** Exact rationals, `HOL-Decision_Procs.Approximation` enclosures, the `round_tie_up` theorem and float-artifact policy (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/swarm/LANE_BRIEF.md:54-55, 119) are arithmetic-specific. VerEKOS's kernels will prove identity, uniqueness, coverage and refusal conditions instead; what transfers is the premise discipline (rule 3), not the interval arithmetic.
- **Oracle7's six admission criteria.** "Stays in lane" and "self stays the core's" are properties of Oracle7's routing constitution (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/PLAN.md:31-34); VerEKOS needs its own criterion set or the scoreboard measures nothing.
- **The relation-store data ceiling and reconstructed index.** 30 absent properties / 1,814 items, the unshipped `names_v1.sqlite3`, and the rebuilt linker index are incident-specific (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/docs/parity/BOARD.md:19, /Users/ember/dev/o7vn/project/HANDOFF-2026-10-18.md:33-35, 77-79). The *practice* — reporting a data gap as a distinct silence class — transfers; the details do not.
- **The performance baseline.** Millisecond binaries vs a 3.6 s / 1.96 GB Python head (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/README.md:62-65) is a comparison against this particular host system.
- **`docs/design/verified-cas.md` is a different sibling's design, not a rulebook.** Its transferable idea is "Anyone may guess; only a proof may answer" — untrusted finder, proved checker, withhold otherwise (/Users/ember/dev/o7vn/docs/design/verified-cas.md:17-24) — which fits a knowledge compiler wherever *checking* a proposed ledger entry is cheaper than *deriving* it; its CAS tables and SymPy panels do not.
