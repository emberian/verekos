# Closeout, 2026-10-19

End of the day: we are pausing the EKOS half here and taking what we learned into minidregg.
This note says what VerEKOS leaves behind, what carries over, and what does not.

## What was built and verified (one day)

- `world/`: a Lean model of a claim-and-receipt ledger (190-odd theorems, standard axioms,
  every build and statement checked by the root). Typeclass carriers; a Mathlib-free
  `WorldCore` behind an enforced import boundary; a minidregg instance whose staleness,
  version events, evidence view and transclusion pins rest on minidregg's own theorems
  (`pinHolds`, `transclusion_pinned`, `toCausalPreimage_injective`, `renderTransclusion`,
  `transclude_uncovered_refused`).
- `map/`: the Lean mapper that produces admitted cells from a compile, byte-identical to
  the Python it replaced; laws as values of minidregg's own `Pred` type.
- `check/`: the runner that makes the model answer scoreboard panels (two panels, zero
  unexplained differences against EKOS) and checks the model's laws on a real run.
- `slice1/`, `reader/`: an EKOS compile landed as cells on hbox with seven receipts (twice),
  and a friend's reader-local compile with live-checked pins.
- A scoreboard with eleven controls over 279 items, one layout, one drift checker.
- Eleven upstream issues (alexeyban/EKOS#10-#20), one defect reproduced empirically.

## What carries over (minidregg-relevant, EKOS-independent)

- **The claim/receipt model** and its minidregg instance: a compiled claim pinned to source
  atoms at revisions, status as a fold over pinned reviews, visibility by pins with deny
  wins, conflicts as records, admission as a ranked pin graph. This is a document-world
  schema for *any* compiled knowledge, not for EKOS.
- **The import family and the law package**: source files as documents with structural-key
  atom ids (a re-import of an unchanged file submits nothing); four `Pred` laws that refuse
  evidence-less births and non-human reviews; shown to hold on a real Host.
- **Two design corrections that only proof produced**: the admission law's second condition
  (re-put may not raise a rank), and the review core as the header revision (a signature
  can return A→B→A; a revision cannot).
- **Process rules now in memory**: no mirrors or twins; the Lean is the thing that runs;
  byte-identity before deletion; a spec is never shipped, only its refinement.

## What does not carry over

- The scoreboard panels and controls are EKOS's unit tests as data. Keep the tooling
  pattern; the data is EKOS's.
- The EKOS findings and the upstream issues stay filed; nothing more is sent.
